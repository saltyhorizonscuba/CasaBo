Casa Bö & Salty Horizon Diving

Guided dives, snorkeling and a first breath underwater in our pool, now arranged with our local dive partner.

October 28, 2026 · Casa Bö

We are happy to announce that Casa Bö is now partnered with Salty Horizon Diving, a local dive operator on the Guanacaste coast. For our guests, this means one simple thing: scuba diving that is easy to book, run by a team we know and trust, and suited to every level, from complete beginners to certified divers.

Why this partnership

Tamarindo and Playa Langosta sit on one of the most beautiful stretches of Costa Rica's Pacific coast. The surf gets the headlines, but the water below has just as much to offer: reefs, rays, turtles and, with some luck, a manta. Many of our guests ask us the same question: where can we dive, and who should we go with? Now we have a clear answer.

What you can book

  • Discover Scuba Diving: an introductory experience for beginners, no certification needed, guided by an instructor.
  • Scuba pool initiation: a first taste of diving in the calm of the villa pool, led by an instructor, before you head out to the ocean.
  • Guided dives for certified divers.
  • Snorkeling trips for those who prefer to stay near the surface.

How it works for Casa Bö guests

Tell our concierge your dates, your experience level and how many of you want to dive. We take care of the coordination with Salty Horizon Diving, so you can spend your holiday in the water instead of on the phone. Non-divers in the group are never left out: the pool, the beach and the rest of our services are right here.

Ready to dive in?

Request your dates and mention scuba in your message. We will arrange the rest.
