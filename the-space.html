The Space

Every corner of Casa Bö is designed to dissolve the boundary between luxury and nature. Explore the spaces that make this home extraordinary.

Your private oasis

A spectacular pool set within a lush tropical garden, the living room mirrors its still, luminous surface. Casa Bö's open-living concept seamlessly dissolves the boundary between architecture and nature.

Where You'll Sleep

Each bedroom is a private retreat with premium linens, Balinese-crafted furnishings, and thoughtful details. Daily housekeeping ensures everything feels fresh and cared for throughout your stay.

The largest of the three rooms, the master suite features a hand-carved Balinese four-poster king bed, private terrace access, and its own en-suite bathroom with rainfall shower.
