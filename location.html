Good to know

How many guests does Casa Bö sleep?

Casa Bö sleeps up to 8 guests in 3 bedrooms, with a private pool, an open-plan kitchen and a living area.

How far is Casa Bö from the beach?

The villa is about 150 metres from Playa Langosta, a short walk. Tamarindo beach and town are a 4-minute drive away.

How far is Casa Bö from Liberia airport (LIR)?

Liberia airport (LIR) is about 1.5 hours by road. San José airport (SJO) is about 4.5 hours.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 pm and check-out is before 10:00 am.

How many bathrooms does Casa Bö have?

Casa Bö has 3 bathrooms, one en-suite for each bedroom, plus an outdoor shower by the pool.

Does Casa Bö have a private pool?

Yes. The villa has its own private pool, along with air conditioning, Wi-Fi, free parking and a fully equipped kitchen.

Is housekeeping included?

Yes. Daily housekeeping is included in your stay.

Can I book a private chef or tours?

Yes. Private chef, concierge and tours can be arranged for your stay. See the Services page for details.

What languages does the host speak?

English, Spanish and French.
