Steps from the beach

Welcome to Casa Bö, a luxurious vacation rental just steps from Playa Langosta and four minutes from Tamarindo's celebrated surf town, home to vibrant bars, refined restaurants, boutiques, spas, and legendary nightlife.

Casa Bö's open-living concept invites you to reconnect with nature while enjoying every comfort of a luxury home. The merged indoor/outdoor space features a spectacular open-plan kitchen, custom-made Balinese furniture, and a living room that mirrors the stillness of a turquoise swimming pool set within a lush tropical garden.
