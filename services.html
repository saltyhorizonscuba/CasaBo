Staff & Services

Our team handles everything, from daily housekeeping to tours and transfers, so you can simply arrive and exhale.

A dedicated team looks after the villa and its guests. Some services are included, others can be arranged on request before or during your stay.

Housekeeping
Included, 4 hours daily (except weekends & statutory holidays)
Concierge
Private chef, activities such as scuba diving, surfing, canopy, wildlife and boat tours. Tamarindo is your gateway to Guanacaste.

Your Concierge

Everything we can arrange for you right at the villa, from the first grocery run to a private dinner. These services come at an additional cost.

Grocery Pre-Stocking

Arrive to a fully stocked kitchen with groceries, wine and essentials ready.

Golf Cart Rental

Rent a golf cart for easy trips to the beach, the restaurants and Tamarindo town.

Airport Transfers

Private transfers from LIR · Liberia (~1.5 hrs) or SJO · San José (~4.5 hrs).

Massage

In-villa massages, by the pool or in the garden.

Scuba Pool Initiation

A first taste of scuba diving in the calm of the villa pool, led by an instructor, before you head out to the ocean.

Pilates & Yoga

Private classes at the villa, by the pool or in the garden.

Private Chef

Available on request, with curated menus prepared in your open-plan kitchen.

Breakfast & Snacks

Simple breakfast and snack options, from fresh fruit to light bites, served by the pool or at your table.

Around Tamarindo

Guided dives and snorkeling trips at Las Catalinas, for first-timers and certified divers.

Surf Lessons

Lessons for every level at Playa Langosta and Tamarindo.

Sail the Pacific coast by catamaran and watch the sun go down from the water.
